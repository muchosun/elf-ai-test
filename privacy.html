Privacy Policy

Updated November 11, 2026 · elf.pink

This page describes how the current Elf preview handles information.

Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides your account identifier, name, email address and basic profile information. Elf uses this information to identify your account and maintain your signed-in session. Elf requests only OpenID, profile and email permissions; it does not request access to Gmail, Drive or your contacts. Your Google password is entered only on Google’s website.

The signed-in session is stored in an encrypted, secure, HTTP-only cookie with a maximum lifetime of 30 days. The application exposes your account ID, name and email to its own interface. Google profile information is not included in the Meta advertising events implemented by this preview.

Information on your device

Email-based preview accounts, password verifiers, onboarding progress and interaction history are stored in your browser. Photos selected for preview stay in browser memory and are not uploaded by this version. Clearing site data removes browser-local information. Signing out clears the server authentication session cookie.

Analytics and hosting

Elf uses Google Analytics and Meta Pixel to measure page visits and funnel events, including registration, checkout starts and checkout returns. These services can receive device, browser, network and event information and use cookies or similar identifiers. Hosting requests are processed by Vercel; example media may load from GitHub Pages. Their respective privacy policies govern their processing.

Checkout

The default checkout is an explicitly labeled test form for synthetic card details. There is no email field and the checkout does not receive your account email. Its fields run in a sandboxed frame without analytics or storage access; page policy blocks network connections and form submission. Input values are cleared and are not passed to analytics. The surrounding page sends only test-stage events (opened, started, all fields filled, validation failed, completed, simulated test payment), a random attempt identifier, selected plan, illustrative price and normal analytics metadata to Google Analytics. Meta Pixel runs on the surrounding page and the cleared result page to measure checkout starts and test completions, with no field values. It cannot read the opaque-origin input frame. These events do not represent a bank transaction. The optional MAVS sandbox opens on the provider’s page and has its own privacy terms.

Your choices and contact

You can sign out, clear Elf site data in your browser and remove Elf’s access from your Google Account connections. For questions or requests concerning information handled by Elf, contact a.svistov@addop.io. Google and Meta provide their own account and privacy controls for data handled by their services.
