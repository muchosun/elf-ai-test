Got Questions?
We’ve Got Answers

What is Elf AI?

Elf is an early product preview for exploring example AI content, templates and characters. AI generation is not connected yet.

How does Elf work?

Choose your interests, browse the examples, and preview the subscription screen. Registration saves your account only in this browser.

What makes Elf different?

This preview lets you explore the proposed creative experience before the product launches.

Is Elf only for content creators?

You can explore the examples whether you create professionally or are simply curious.

What type of content can I generate on Elf?

The catalog contains illustrative photos, videos and characters. Generating new content is not available in this preview.

Do I need technical skills to use Elf?

No technical setup is required to browse the preview.

Is it safe to upload my photo to Elf AI?

Selected photos stay on your device and are not uploaded. Reloading clears selected photos.

Can Elf create realistic AI images of me if I have tattoos?

Tattoo fidelity and other generation features will be evaluated when generation is available.
